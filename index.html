THE GRADUATION CHRONICLE

✦ ✦ ✦
CLASS OF 2026 • SENIOR EDITION
“Our Voices, Our Future, Our Legacy.”
ARTICLE 1

Saber Test Stories

  • Preparing for the ICFES: Study, Practice and Teamwork: Preparing for the ICFES requires practice and focus. Working in groups helps students explain tough topics, reduce stress, and protect their mental health for better results.
  • Practical Tips: Offers actionable advice for exam day, including reviewing key concepts, getting enough sleep, managing time wisely, reading carefully, and staying calm.
  • Managing Pressure & Expectations: Reminds students that standardized test scores do not define their personal worth, encouraging them to manage anxiety, pace themselves, and stay confident.
ARTICLE 2

Our Future

  • OUR FUTURE: Focused on Dreams, Goals and the Journey to Adulthood: The text highlights the transition from high school to university and the working world. It reflects on post-graduation ambitions, emphasizing that leaving school marks the beginning of a new chapter filled with personal growth, teamwork, and determination to build a bright future.
  • Fear vs. Excitement: Captures the mixed emotions of graduation—leaving familiar memories behind while stepping confidently toward college, careers, and personal dreams.
  • Transition and Independence: Explores the shift from school to adult life, focusing on gaining independence, making decisions, and embracing new opportunities.
ARTICLE 3

OBITUARIES

  • From Big Backpacks to Dreams in Motion: A reflective journey from childhood memories with heavy backpacks to graduation day, celebrating personal growth, friendships, and future dreams.
  • Humor/Jokes: A humorous tribute to sleep lost since 9th grade, late-night group chats, instant coffee, and broken sleep promises.
  • Tribute to Our Teachers: A heartfelt tribute honoring our teachers for their guidance, patience, and lasting life lessons beyond academics.
